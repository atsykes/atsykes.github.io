64-bit SIMD processor and microarchitecture study

A 4-stage pipelined vector processor in synthesizable Verilog. Its single-cycle divider forced every instruction onto a roughly 100 ns clock, so a partner and I compared five redesigns. My Version C, a multi-cycle divide unit plus a scoreboard, was the fastest.

Version C
Up to 15.8× faster than the baseline, best of 5 designs, for 1.6% more area than the version without the scoreboard
Clock
About 100 ns down to 1.84 ns after moving DIV, MOD and SQRT into a 64-cycle SFU
Forwarding
My EX/WB-to-ID forwarding cut baseline cycles 19%, verified by 20,024 hazard checks
My role
Baseline co-designed with a partner. I built the forwarding, the SFU, the scoreboard, and the combined Version E.
